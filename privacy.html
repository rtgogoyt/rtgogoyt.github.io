Privacy Policy

Kantudongqian Uploader · Last updated 2026-11-23

Kantudongqian Uploader ("the app") is an internal tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel 看片懂錢 (Kantudongqian). It is used only by the channel owner to manage the channel's own videos.

Data the app accesses

With the channel owner's authorization through Google OAuth, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos to the owner's channel and to read or update the metadata of those videos (title, description, tags, thumbnail, captions, privacy status, publish time and synthetic-media disclosure). It reads the owner's channel ID to confirm which channel it is connected to.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish and manage the owner's own videos. OAuth tokens are stored privately in the owner's own storage and are never shared. The app does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other user, does not use data for advertising, and does not transfer data to third parties.

YouTube and Google

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

Deletion

The owner can delete stored tokens at any time, and revoking access in Google account settings invalidates them immediately.

Contact

Please contact the channel owner through the YouTube channel.
