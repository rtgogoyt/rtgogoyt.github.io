「kanpian-publisher」是「看片懂錢」頻道自用的發文工具，只用來把我們自己製作的影片與文字發佈到我們自己的 Facebook 粉絲專頁、Instagram 與 Threads 帳號。

  • 如需刪除資料或有任何問題，請透過 Facebook 粉絲專頁「看片懂錢」（https://www.facebook.com/kanpiandongqian）私訊聯絡我們。

kanpian-publisher is a private publishing tool used only by the 看片懂錢 channel to post its own content to its own Facebook Page, Instagram and Threads accounts. It does not collect, store or share any personal data of other users. For data deletion or questions, contact us via the Facebook Page above.
